# About DrugIQ

DrugIQ is a browser-based research workspace for exploring the public scientific record. It brings literature, biological targets, compounds, biomarkers and clinical trials into a connected interface. Its eleven specialist tools can be used individually, while the Candidate Dossier brings several evidence views together around a target, compound and indication. It is intended for discovery researchers, translational researchers, and people learning how these research questions connect.

## What the workspace does

The interface provides example inputs, a shared disease context, Guide Mode explanations, source panels and research-result exports. Different tools use public-data lookups, AI-assisted interpretation and links to independently operated research resources. Availability depends on the selected workflow and its upstream services. A generated statement is not automatically a verified fact: inspect the cited record, distinguish source data from interpretation, and check whether any evidence probe was unavailable.

## Research, not clinical decision-making

DrugIQ is for research and exploration. It is not a diagnostic service, treatment recommendation system, emergency service or validated predictor of clinical success. Some scores in the current application are heuristics. References to external models do not mean those models execute inside DrugIQ. The [limitations guide](/docs/limitations) explains these boundaries in more detail.
