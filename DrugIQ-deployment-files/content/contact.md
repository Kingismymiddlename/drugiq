# Contact DrugIQ

This page describes the contact options that are actually available for DrugIQ. The supplied application does not contain a verified maintainer email, support telephone number, mailing address or public issue tracker. A monitored support channel has therefore not been claimed or invented. At present, this page is an information page, not a message-submission service, and no message is delivered by reading it.

## Finding help now

For instructions about choosing a research tool, required inputs and interpreting results, use the [DrugIQ documentation](/docs) and the [research limitations guide](/docs/limitations). The assistant in the [workspace](https://drugiq.vercel.app/#welcome) can help with navigation, but it is not a human support desk and should not be used to submit confidential information, complaints requiring a formal response, or privacy-rights requests.

## Reporting an issue responsibly

Once the operator publishes a monitored contact channel, a useful report should include the tool name, the approximate time of the problem, the browser used, and a reproducible example made from public or synthetic information. Do not include patient records, proprietary compound structures, passwords, access tokens or unredacted screenshots. For an incorrect scientific interpretation, record the source identifier and the disputed statement so the underlying evidence can be checked.

## Operator information still needed

The maintainer needs to publish a verified contact email and the identity responsible for operating this site. A business address should be published only when it is accurate and appropriate to make public. Until these details are confirmed, DrugIQ does not claim a response-time commitment, a formal privacy-request channel or a verified business location. See [privacy and data handling](/privacy) for the current disclosure and its limits.
